Good to know

Can I come and see the place first?
Yes, and we would prefer it. Send the enquiry form or call +91 99982 02849 and we will show you around at a time that suits you. There is no charge and no obligation.
What is the shortest term I can take?
A single day. Day passes suit people who are in Ahmedabad for a short trip or just need a quiet place for an afternoon. Most members take a monthly plan because it works out cheaper.
What is included in the monthly price?
The desk or cabin, furniture, electricity, high-speed internet, housekeeping, pantry access and use of the common areas. Conference room hours are bundled into most plans. There are no hidden maintenance charges.
Is there parking?
Shilp Arcade sits directly on S.P. Ring Road next to the HP petrol pump, with parking at the complex. Give us a call before you visit and we will tell you exactly where to pull in.
